There are recurring legal problems among citizens in India. Citizens come across legal cases, tenant disputes, consumer grievances, harassment at work, cyber fraud, or even legal matters related to domestic life without having any knowledge of their rights, legal provisions, and measures to be taken. Legal documents are complex and not easy to understand for an ordinary citizen while generic AI Chatbots give ambiguous and incorrect answers.
Know Your Rights, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation system will answer everyday legal questions of users in simple and plain language. It has been developed by combining an expertly curated knowledge base of Indian laws and provisions with a large language model that gives accurate and contextual responses. The system when asked about a certain situation, retrieves the relevant references to the laws and explains the legal rights of the user with the appropriate act or section along with practical measures to be taken.
The project follows a structured pipeline: data collection and curation from reliable legal sources, text preprocessing and indexing, retrieval using FAISS vector search, and response generation through a local language model. The system is designed with a focus on women's legal protections, covering areas such as workplace harassment, domestic violence, and dowry-related offenses, while also addressing general consumer and tenancy rights.
Key evaluation metrics include retrieval accuracy, response relevance, and factual correctness against a test set of common legal queries. The project demonstrates an end-to-end application of retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge representation, and responsible AI practices—ensuring that legal information is delivered accurately, accessibly, and without hallucination.
